Research Overview

MSRCR v2 brings compliant modules, adaptable connections, and reconfiguration planning into one robotic platform. Each unit combines a bending continuum backbone with grippers that support both docking and object handling. Reorganizing these shared building blocks produces different body forms and motion strategies.

The study connects morphology to behavior through a bioinspired gait library and develops a geometric-topological representation for planning changes in configuration. Physical demonstrations combine locomotion, reconfiguration, and manipulation, showing how modular compliance supports interaction with constrained environments.

Read the published article for the full abstract, methods, results, and supplementary movies.

Robot Design

Compliant backbone

Tendon actuation bends the continuum body; compliant joints allow the module to adapt during contact.

Grasping and docking

Dual-purpose grippers support object manipulation and quasi-freeform connections between modules.

Reconfiguration planning

A unified representation combines connectivity and geometry to plan grasping, release, and deformation actions.
